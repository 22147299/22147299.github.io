Internet Service Porivder

A brief description

An ISP (Internet Service Provider) is a company that provides individuals and businesses with access to the internet. Think of it as a gateway, or a bridge, that connects your device to the global network. By maintaining large networks of equipment and cables, ISPs route your data back and forth from your device to the "internet backbone," allowing you to browse websites, send emails, stream content, and use other online services.

There are four significant types of ISP connections: DSL, fiber-optic, satellite, and mobile broadband. Each connection type has its advantages and disadvantages. For example, speeds for fiber-optic are faster but cost more, whereas DSL costs cheaper but takes longer to load.

The first recognized commercial Internet Service Provider (ISP) was The World, which began serving customers in November 1989. However, Telenet, introduced in 1974 as a commercial version of ARPANET, is often cited as the first ISP in the context of ARPANET, though not for the internet as we know it today.
